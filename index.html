01 — The Practice

Four disciplines, one standard.

Every engagement begins from a single premise — the machine adapts to you, never the reverse.

Bespoke Model Training

A private model trained on your corpus alone — your writing, your decisions, your reference material. Nothing shared, nothing diluted.

Private Knowledge Curation

Your correspondence, library and archive are structured into a living corpus your AI draws upon — searchable, discreet, permanent.

Taste & Judgment

Calibrated to your standards: how you write, what you approve, what you would never. The model learns the difference.

Lifetime Refinement

As your practice evolves, your AI is recalibrated — quietly, on schedule, for as long as you keep it.

02 — The Method

From consultation to a mind of your own.

Four measured steps. No templates, no shortcuts.

  1. 01

    The Consultation

    A private conversation about how you think, decide and work. We map what your AI must know — and how it must behave.

  2. 02

    The Curation

    Your materials are gathered, structured and refined into a training corpus — handled discreetly and encrypted end to end.

  3. 03

    The Training

    Your model is trained on isolated, private infrastructure. Nothing leaves your control at any stage.

  4. 04

    The Refinement

    Calibration against your feedback, quarter after quarter, keeps the model aligned with your judgment — for life.

03 — Privacy

Yours, entirely.

Privacy is not a feature of this practice. It is the practice. Every engagement is engineered so that your intelligence — and yours alone — benefits from your knowledge.

The model we build for you is not a product. It is a possession.

No public client list. No case studies. No exceptions.

i

Private infrastructure

Training and hosting on dedicated, client-isolated hardware. Never shared, never pooled.

ii

Nothing trains anyone else

Your corpus never touches shared models, public datasets or other clients. Not now, not ever.

iii

You own the weights

The trained model and its corpus remain your property — archived, exportable or destroyed at your word.

iv

Discretion as standard

Minimal metadata, private billing, no public client list. We are trusted because we are quiet.
